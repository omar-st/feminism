Feminism is a political and social movement which aims to establish equality between the sexes. This means equal access to political, economic, and personal rights and opportunities. What feminists want is for men and women, boys and girls, to have as many rights and resources as each other. Feminism is a movement for liberation, because this equality is achieved by removing restrictions from women rather than adding them to men. Anyone can be a feminist. You are one if you believe we deserve a fair and just world.

The feminist movement has been active throughout the world since the 1800s. The struggle has been unending, and if you observe the attitudes of some people today, you might think that no progress has been made in 200 years. However, nothing could be further from the truth. The world we live in today would be unrecognizble to the billions of people who lived and died before us.

Our History

Throughout our recorded history and across human societies, women have been denied rights and opportunities that men enjoyed. There is evidence that this has not always been true for all humans, as some hunter gatherers experience much more social equality than in our societies. However, in every society where we see hierarchies of wealth and power, we also see what is described as "Patriarchy". This is where the top of the social structure is reserved for men, and the interests of these men are prioritized in society.

Across countries and continents, women were excluded from political participation. Almost no women had access to education. They could be treated as property, transferred from their father to their husband. Often they would have no say in who that husband was. There was no protection from domestic violence, and often it was glorified. A woman usually could not have her own home independently. In almost all cultures, children belonged to the father's family. The idea that the mother might ever keep custody of the children is new - 20th century new.

Yet despite this, today we can see that these restrictions were stupid. How do we know that? Because in the modern world, we have removed these limitations, and the world did not end. In fact, it proved how wrong we were. Historical people thought that women were, by and large, not smart enough to educate. That they could never learn to read or write.

Fast forward to today: Over 90% of all human beings can read. Male and female. In countries with strong public education, it's over 99% of kids, boys and girls. Today, slightly more women than men graduate from college.

A sophisticated man in 1700, while experimenting with electricity and separating elements, might have looked down on his wife and daughters. He might have assumed that women in general were born illiterate. (Like everyone?) That they could never be smart enough for schooling. Look around today, and it's clear he had a lot to learn.

Our Future

But today we are so much more enlightened, right? Here in the 21st century, we have moved past all of our false assumptions, like that women can't read. So we only have true assumptions left, right? That women are more emotional and better suited to cooking, cleaning, and children; while men are suited to leadership, reverence, and never washing their laundry.

I don't know. What if the revolution is only getting started? Across most cultures for thousands of years, when kids were born they belonged to their father's family. But today, if you looked at the cases of sole custody, you would never guess that. Something can be normal for thousands of years, and then change. And after the change, we never realize the world was ever different in the past.

Right now, we have our own 21st century sexism. And it's light and delicate compared to 18th century sexism. But its beliefs are no less false. No less invented. No less dependent on the particular culture we happen to have. The greatest lesson of history is that things don't have to be This Way. It wasn't like this in the past; it won't be like this in the future. It's up to us to decide what that future WILL be.

Some people believe that men and women cannot be friends. However, this is completely untrue. All human beings are equal. All of us are capable of compassionate friendships built on respect, and our race, religion, sect, or sex has no impact on this.
