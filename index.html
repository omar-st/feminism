Feminism is a political and social movement, which aims to establish equality between the sexes. This means equal access to political, economic, and personal rights and opportunities. What feminists want is that men and women, boys and girls, have as many rights and resources as each other. Feminism is a movement for liberation, because this equality is achieved by removing restrictions from women rather than adding them to men. Anyone can be a feminist. You are one if you believe we deserve a fair and just world.

The feminist movement has been active throughout the world since the 1800s. The struggle has been unending, and if you observe the attitudes of some people today, you might think that no progress has been made in 200 years. However, nothing could be further from the truth. The world we live in today would be unrecognizble to the billions of people who lived and died before us.
